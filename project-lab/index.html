Project Lab is a space for original applications, working prototypes, and technical experiments. Its purpose is to show what a product can do, not serve as a directory of public code repositories.

The approach

Explore

Start with a problem, a question, or an unexplored possibility.

Build

Turn an idea into something interactive and technically substantial.

Validate

Test the experience, improve what matters, and share only what is ready.

The project lineup is being refined. Individual applications and demonstrations will be featured here as their scope, testing, and presentation are ready.
