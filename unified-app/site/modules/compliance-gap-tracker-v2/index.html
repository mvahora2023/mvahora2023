About this demonstration

What this is: A browser-only portfolio demonstration of the reporting interface a cybersecurity compliance team might use. Keystone Gas & Electric, staff names, status records and reports are fictional.

What this is not: A real-time monitoring platform, legal interpretation of TSA / PUC obligations, or evidence of actual utility controls. Requirements are mock categories, not authoritative regulatory language.

Data model: The demo generates 18 TSA-labeled and 12 PUC-labeled sample requirements, and defines 11 example open exceptions. The compliance percentage equals the count of fully compliant rows divided by all requirement rows. Overdue exceptions use an illustrative 90-day threshold. The high-risk count includes exceptions marked High.

Trend data: February–July 2026 series are deliberately invented display examples. They are not forecasts, independent measurements, or a continuation of a live data feed.

Technical design: Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; custom Canvas visualizations; responsive table filtering and CSV export; local/offline support. No login or third-party tracking scripts.
